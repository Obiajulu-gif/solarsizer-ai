# Demo video script (3–5 minutes)

Judges treat the demo video as the source of truth for what was built. Show the real app, not slides.
Suggested file name: `TeamName_Brief07_Demo.mp4` (MP4, 1080p or lower, under 500 MB).

1. **0:00–0:20 Who and why.** "Mama Ngozi runs a provisions shop in Lagos. She spends about ₦25,000 a week on
   generator petrol."
2. **0:20–1:10 Describe it.** Type "1 freezer, 2 standing fans, a TV and 6 bulbs", pick Lagos, enter 25000,
   click *Size my system*. Show the appliance list the AI read, and change one value to show it is editable.
3. **1:10–2:00 The answer.** Inverter, battery and panels; the cost breakdown; the payback month on the chart.
4. **2:00–2:40 Brief 7 metric.** Litres of petrol and tonnes of CO₂ avoided per year, money saved, the 5-year
   figure. Change the petrol price per litre to show the numbers respond.
5. **2:40–3:10 Affordability.** The monthly payment against today's petrol bill.
6. **3:10–3:40 Location.** Switch Lagos to Kano: sun hours change, the panel count drops.
7. **3:40–4:10 Low connectivity.** Share on WhatsApp (plain text). Turn off the network, reload, enter
   appliances by hand: it still works.
8. **4:10–4:40 What we built this weekend.** State clearly what existed before Oct 30 and what was added during
   the sprint (from `CHANGELOG.md`).
